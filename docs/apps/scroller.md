The **Scroller App** lets you control your phone semi-hands-free - no touching the screen!  
Scroll, like, and pause videos with just a tap of a button on your BLEShark Nano.  
This is perfect for:
- Eating while watching
- Remote control from across the room
### How to Use

To get started:

<ol className="step-list">
  <li>Navigate to the <strong>Apps</strong> menu on your BLEShark Nano.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Scroller</strong>.</li>
  <li>On your phone, pair with the BLEShark Nano - the device name is shown on its screen.</li>
</ol>

Here’s how each button works:

| Button         | Action                |
|----------------|------------------------|
| [L] / [R] | Scroll up / down        |
| [S]               | Pause / Unpause         |
| [S] (Double Click) | Like the current video  |

The main Scroller screen also shows the hint <em>"Hold S+R to modify cursor"</em>, which switches the device into Modify Mode (see below).

### Modify Mode

Modify Mode turns the Scroller into a rough cursor pad. It's useful when you need to nudge the pointer on your phone, for example to tap a button or close a popup, without switching apps or picking up the device.

To enter Modify Mode, hold <strong>[S]</strong> and <strong>[R]</strong> together for about half a second from the main Scroller screen. The screen switches to a page titled <strong>Modify Mode</strong>.

While in Modify Mode:

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| [L] | Move cursor up and to the left |
| [R] | Move cursor down and to the right |
| [S] | Exit back to the normal scroll screen |

Modify Mode is the fastest way to dismiss a popup or reposition the pointer mid-scroll without leaving the Scroller app.

:::caution[Heads-up!]
If you’ve previously paired your BLEShark Nano using the **Bad-BT** app, you may need to **unpair it first**.  
That mode registers the device as a keyboard instead of a mouse, which disables scrolling functions.
:::
